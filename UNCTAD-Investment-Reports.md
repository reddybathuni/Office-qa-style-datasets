# UNCTAD World Investment Reports (2014-2023)

This dataset comprises the UNCTAD World Investment Reports published from 2014 to 2023. These extensive global macroeconomic reports focus on Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) trends and feature complex data tables comparing various global regions.
