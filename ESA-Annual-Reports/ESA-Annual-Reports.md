# ESA Space Debris Environment Report

Published annually by ESA's Space Debris Office since 2017, it provides a comprehensive overview of global space activity and assesses how well international debris-reduction measures are supporting the long-term sustainability of spaceflight. The report is packed with facts, figures, and tables detailing how the space debris environment around Earth has evolved. It covers tracked object counts, launch traffic statistics, fragmentation events, debris density plots, and end-of-life disposal compliance — all heavily numerical with time-series charts spanning the entire space age.
