# World Economic Forum (WEF) Global Risks Reports (2015-2024)

This dataset compiles the annual Global Risks Reports published by the World Economic Forum from 2015 to 2024. These comprehensive documents analyze and forecast the most severe global risks across economic, environmental, geopolitical, societal, and technological categories.
