# Bank for International Settlements (BIS) Annual Reports (2015-2024)

This dataset provides the official Bank for International Settlements (BIS) Annual Economic Reports spanning Fiscal Years 2015 through 2024. These comprehensive documents detail global macroeconomic trends, central bank monetary policy analysis, and the financial activities of the BIS.
