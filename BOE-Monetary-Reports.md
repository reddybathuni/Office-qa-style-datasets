# Bank of England Monetary Policy Reports (2014-2023)

This dataset compiles the November editions of the Bank of England's Monetary Policy Reports (formerly known as Inflation Reports) from 2014 to 2023. These documents offer essential insights into the UK's economic outlook, inflation projections, and the Monetary Policy Committee's interest rate decisions.
